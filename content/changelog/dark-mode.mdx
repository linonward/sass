---
title: Dark mode everywhere
date: 2026-09-24
category: feature
---

Every page now follows your system theme, and the toggle in the header remembers your choice. The whole palette is derived from the brand colour in `site.config.ts`, so dark mode needs no extra configuration — put your own colour in and both themes follow.

- Marketing pages, the dashboard and the admin area all switch together
- Email templates stay light: dark mode in mail clients is still unpredictable
